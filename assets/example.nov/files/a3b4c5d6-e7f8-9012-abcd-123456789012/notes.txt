Need to establish clear rules for what each crystal type can and CANNOT do. Sanderson's Laws of Magic apply here — the reader should be able to predict what's possible before Kira figures it out.

Balance considerations:
- Aether/Opal should be so rare that using it is a major plot event (maybe only 2-3 times in the whole novel)
- Crystal Burn rate should be proportional to the power used — small effects barely drain, but the Shattered Crown nearly killed Malachar instantly
- Kira shouldn't master all seven types. She naturally attunes to Aquor (water) due to her emotional connection to water, and later learns Lumin (light). Let her struggle with Ignis (fire) — she never quite gets it.

The Shattered Crown:
- Was an artifact made from all seven crystal types in perfect harmonic balance
- Its purpose was to seal an ancient entity called "the Silence" — a force of absolute nothingness
- When the Crown shattered, the seal weakened. The labyrinth formed around the Crown's remnants as a secondary containment
- Restoring the Crown will reseal the Silence, but destroy the labyrinth and everyone inside
