The three paths should each teach Kira something about the corresponding element before she can proceed deeper. The water path will force her to confront her fear directly — perhaps a submerged chamber she must swim through.

The ruby/fire path will be explored later in Chapter 4 when she returns. The emerald/earth path connects to the World Tree in Chapter 7.

Each path should have its own "trial" — water tests courage, fire tests passion, earth tests patience. Kira needs to pass all three before the Guardian will let her through.
