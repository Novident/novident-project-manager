Act 1: The Awakening
Kira enters the labyrinth, discovers the crystal paths, meets the Guardian. Ends with the revelation about the Shattered Crown.

Act 2: The Weaving
Kira learns to attune to crystals. She must navigate all three elemental paths. Malachar is introduced as both antagonist and reluctant mentor. Midpoint: Kira discovers her mother's fate.

Act 3: The Crown
Kira must choose: restore the Crown and destroy the labyrinth, or claim its power and risk the Silence breaking free. The climax involves a harmonic resonance that requires all seven crystal types working in concert.
