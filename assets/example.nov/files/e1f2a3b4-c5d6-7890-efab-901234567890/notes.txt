Malachar must never be purely villainous. The key to writing him is that his logic is sound — he's not wrong about the danger, he's wrong about the solution. Readers should occasionally find themselves agreeing with him before remembering what he's willing to sacrifice.

Voice notes:
- Speaks formally, like someone from a much older era
- Uses crystal metaphors constantly ("even the purest crystal has its flaws")
- Rarely raises his voice — his anger is cold and precise
- When he's close to lucidity, he uses contractions (showing his "human" side breaking through)

Physical description:
- His body is partially crystallized — one arm and part of his torso have been replaced by translucent crystal
- The crystallization is spreading slowly (this is his ticking clock — he's running out of time)
- His remaining human eye is grey; his crystal eye glows faintly blue
